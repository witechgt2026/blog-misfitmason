Volatility Explained

Volatility, or variance, measures the risk involved in a slot game. Here's what you need to know:

  • Low Volatility: Frequent but smaller wins.
  • Medium Volatility: A balance between win frequency and size.
  • High Volatility: Less frequent but larger wins.

Understanding volatility can help you manage your bankroll effectively. If you're in it for the long haul, high volatility games can be thrilling, but they require patience.

Bonus-Buy Mechanics

Bonus-buy features allow players to purchase bonus rounds directly, skipping the base game. This can be a tempting option, but it's not without its caveats:

  • Costly: Bonus-buys can be expensive, often 100 times your bet.
  • Quick Access: Skip straight to the action without waiting for a bonus round.
